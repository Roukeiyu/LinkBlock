LinkBlock 第 5–10 关 · Suno 配乐创作稿

状态：创作提示词，尚未在 Suno 生成音频，也尚未替换游戏中的音乐。

整体方向
延续羊皮纸、手绘怪物和夜色场景的暗黑幻想气质。旋律要清楚、有记忆点，
配器有变化，但给画线、受击和 Boss 预警音效留下空间。避免整曲只有低频
嗡鸣和机械循环，也避免电影预告片式的大鼓、突然爆音和过度宏大的编曲。

使用方式
在 https://suno.com/create 的自定义创作中，将对应英文 Style 文本填入风格描述。
选择纯器乐（Instrumental），歌词留空。若有排除风格字段，可使用公共排除项。
每曲目标约 2–3 分钟。时长、节拍和循环结尾是创作目标，生成后需要实际试听，
不能仅凭提示词认定达成。标题使用下方中文名或英文名均可。

公共排除项（若界面提供 Exclude styles）
vocals, singing, spoken word, choir, EDM drops, trap hi-hats, distorted sub bass,
trailer braams, jump scares, shrill bells, harsh cymbals, long ambient intro,
fade-out ending, sound effects

交付处理
选定版本后保存 Suno 曲目链接、生成日期和原始音频。随后试听并裁切循环点、
统一六曲的主观响度、检查循环接缝，导出 Opus/OGG 与 MP3 两种格式，使用
新的资源名接入关卡以避开旧缓存，并验证暂停、换关、音量设置和手机播放。
生成素材的来源信息将记录在游戏 AUDIO.txt 中，不标成程序合成或 CC0 素材。

05 · 秋夜南瓜谷 / Lanterns on the Hollow Road
角色：无头骑士
目标：有疾驰感的秋夜冒险，神秘而有旋律，避免滑稽万圣节音效。
节奏：约 96 BPM，6/8 律动。
主配器：中提琴、低音弦乐、拨弦、木管和轻框鼓。

Style:
Instrumental dark fantasy chamber game soundtrack, 96 BPM, lilting 6/8 gallop,
D minor. A memorable four-bar viola melody answers a warm bassoon, supported by
pizzicato cello, muted strings, wooden percussion and gentle frame drum. Riding
through an autumn valley lit by pumpkin lanterns, mysterious, bittersweet and
quietly adventurous. Natural acoustic timbres, intimate ensemble, expressive
melody, restrained bass. Begin with the musical pulse immediately. Develop the
theme with a woodwind variation, a brief softer passage, then a fuller return;
maintain a steady gameplay groove for about 2–3 minutes. Leave space for game
sound effects. End with a short turnaround back into the opening harmony,
steady tempo and similar energy, no fade. Entirely instrumental.

06 · 沉潮王庭 / Beneath the Moonlit Tide
角色：海妖
目标：水下遗迹的流动感和忧郁旋律，不能只是空旷氛围铺底。
节奏：约 78 BPM，柔和 6/8 律动。
主配器：竖琴、低音长笛、大提琴、柔和钢琴与轻手鼓。

Style:
Instrumental underwater fantasy game soundtrack, 78 BPM, flowing 6/8, A minor
with gentle Dorian color. Rolling harp arpeggios carry a lyrical bass flute
melody, answered by warm cello and soft felt piano. Rounded hand percussion
and a restrained pulsing bass suggest slow ocean currents. Moonlight through
the ruins of a sunken court, haunting but inviting, clear melody and emotional
warmth. Acoustic chamber ensemble, controlled spacious reverb, detailed middle
register. Introduce the theme within the first bars; vary its orchestration
every 8–16 bars, with a modest tension lift and a calm return. About 2–3 minutes,
stable dynamics for active gameplay. Finish on a looping harmonic turnaround
at the opening tempo, no fade. Pure instrumental, no voices or ocean effects.

07 · 狐火神社 / Lanterns Beyond the Torii
角色：狐妖
目标：夜间神社的灵巧与幻术感；和风配器融入同一套室内乐音色。
节奏：约 100 BPM，4/4。
主配器：筝、尺八、拨奏弦乐、小鼓与柔和低音。

Style:
Instrumental Japanese-inspired dark fantasy chamber game music, 100 BPM, 4/4,
D minor pentatonic. A graceful, memorable koto motif trades phrases with a
low breathy shakuhachi, supported by pizzicato strings, soft cello and light
wooden percussion. Small taiko accents used sparingly. Foxfire flickers around
an old moonlit shrine: elusive, playful in rhythm, elegant and faintly ominous.
Warm natural instruments, subtle syncopation, spacious clear mix, melodic rather
than ambient. Start with the koto hook, add a contrasting flute phrase, then
return with interwoven strings. Around 2–3 minutes of steady exploration and
combat flow, no dramatic breaks. Bring the last phrase back toward the opening
ostinato for looping, no fade. Entirely instrumental, no chants or vocals.

08 · 极光冰座 / Aurora over the Frozen Lake
角色：冰霜巨人
目标：冷峻、开阔、有重量，同时保留可记忆的温柔旋律。
节奏：约 76 BPM，4/4。
主配器：低音大提琴、毡音钢琴、中提琴、低木管和轻鼓。

Style:
Instrumental Nordic dark fantasy chamber soundtrack for a game, 76 BPM, 4/4,
B minor. A broad cello melody rises over softly pulsing low strings, felt piano
and warm viola harmonies, with sparse low wooden flute replies. Gentle frame
drum creates a patient, weighty heartbeat. Vast frozen lake beneath aurora,
an ancient giant, solitude with quiet courage. Cold spacious atmosphere but a
warm, singable instrumental theme; no piercing high notes. Begin with motion
and melody, introduce a tender piano variation, then return to the cello theme
with fuller strings. About 2–3 minutes, consistent moderate dynamics and room
for gameplay cues. Keep tempo through the ending and resolve into the starting
harmonic pattern for a smooth loop, no fade. No human voices.

09 · 暮钟圣城 / Shadows in the Belfry
角色：石像鬼
目标：哥特钟楼的悬疑与肃穆；避免教堂合唱掩盖游戏提示音。
节奏：约 90 BPM，3/4。
主配器：柔和管风琴、弦乐、低木管和少量低钟音。

Style:
Instrumental gothic chamber game soundtrack, 90 BPM, a restrained 3/4 dark
waltz in G minor. A memorable viola and cello theme moves over soft pipe-organ
chords, bass clarinet and light plucked strings. Sparse mellow low bell notes
mark the harmony, never loud or sharp. A ruined cathedral at dusk, stone wings
above a silent city, solemn mystery with forward momentum. Intimate, melodic,
warm and weathered rather than grand cinematic horror. Clear midrange and
controlled cathedral reverb. Theme, woodwind variation, gently intensifying
string counterpoint, return; about 2–3 minutes with no long empty introduction.
End with the opening accompaniment returning at equal energy for looping,
no fade or final crash. Strictly instrumental, no choir or chanting.

10 · 熔冠终庭 / Embers beneath the Crown
角色：炎魔
目标：终关更有推进力和紧张感，但保持旋律与克制的响度。
节奏：约 112 BPM，4/4。
主配器：弦乐固定音型、圆号、中提琴、大提琴和低鼓。

Style:
Instrumental dark fantasy final-stage game music, 112 BPM, 4/4, D minor with
occasional Phrygian tension. Driving low-string ostinato and measured frame
drums support a strong, memorable horn and viola melody. Cello counterlines,
restrained timpani and dark woodwinds build urgency without overwhelming the
theme. Ancient volcanic ruins, drifting embers, a powerful guardian, resolve
and danger. Organic chamber-orchestral sound, warm low mids, controlled bass
and percussion, no trailer bombast. Begin in motion; develop a heroic variation,
a short quieter tension passage, then a richer thematic return. Around 2–3
minutes at steady tempo with space for attack warning sounds. Finish with a
turnaround into the opening ostinato, no fade or abrupt final hit. No vocals.
