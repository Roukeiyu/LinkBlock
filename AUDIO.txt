封林 audio
============

Background music
----------------
Each level loops one track. Levels 1–5 use music supplied by the repository owner;
levels 6–10 use original synthesized ambient loops made for this expansion. The game
prefers the Opus .ogg and falls back to .mp3. A file is downloaded only
after the first tap or key press, and only for the level being played.

These new 16-bar stereo loops are synthesized without third-party samples.
Their tempos, roots and melodies differ; note and reverb tails wrap around
the loop boundary. Rebuild them with scripts/build-expansion-assets.py
(Python 3, NumPy and ffmpeg with libopus/libmp3lame). Normal builds use the
committed audio files and do not require Python or ffmpeg.

Sound effects
-------------
Every effect (drawing, capture, the seal, the boss, UI clicks) is still
synthesized at runtime by src/audio.ts with the Web Audio API. Those
effects are original and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
