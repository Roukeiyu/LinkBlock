封林 audio
============

Background music
----------------
Title: 月夜森林 Moonlit Grove
File: public/audio/moonlit-grove.ogg (Opus, about 96 kbps VBR) and
      public/audio/moonlit-grove.mp3 (128 kbps fallback)
Length: 3:19
Source: generated by the repository owner, roukeiyu, on their own Suno
account (Suno id 0bcc47c6-393b-4e6c-ad0b-e7c9d5b8aa77, created
2026-11-10). The shipped files are web encodes of that export, with the
cover image removed. The game loops whichever format the browser can
play, and does not download it until the first tap or key press.

Sound effects
-------------
Every effect (drawing, capture, the seal, the boss, UI clicks) is still
synthesized at runtime by src/audio.ts with the Web Audio API. Those
effects are original and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The Suno track is the owner's own generation for this game. It is not
covered by the CC0 dedication above.
