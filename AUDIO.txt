封林 audio
============

Background music
----------------
Each level loops one track. Levels 1–4 retain the owner-supplied music.
Levels 5–10 use Suno recordings generated for the owner and prepared as loops. The game
prefers the Opus .ogg and falls back to .mp3. A file is downloaded only
after the first tap or key press, and only for the level being played.

These six recordings are Suno-generated, not programmatically synthesized and
not covered by the CC0 sound-effect dedication. Generation dates were not
included with the downloaded source metadata. Original MP3 downloads are
retained separately; source SHA-256 hashes and output measurements are in
public/audio/suno-v1-sources.json.

Processing: trim detected terminal silence below -45 dB, overlap the final and
initial 3 seconds with an equal-power crossfade, and rotate the loop to start
at the original 3-second point. Two-pass loudness normalization targets -20 LUFS
with a -2 dBTP ceiling, followed by stereo 48 kHz Opus (96 kbps) and MP3 (160 kbps)
exports. A 10 ms de-click envelope at each endpoint prevents codec-boundary
clicks without a musical fade-out. Final measurements are recorded per encoded format in the manifest.
No pitch or tempo correction is applied. Musical phrasing, instrumentation,
absence of voices and subjective loop quality have not been audition-verified;
automated decoding, loudness and playback checks do not establish those facts.

Rebuild from approved original downloads and their sources.json:
  python3 scripts/prepare-suno-audio.py /path/to/originals
Requires Python 3, NumPy and ffmpeg/ffprobe with libopus/libmp3lame. Normal game builds
use the committed exports and need neither Python nor ffmpeg. New -suno-v1
resource names avoid stale cached audio; levels 1–4 and sound effects are unchanged.

Legacy unused audio retained for history: autumn-ride was the owner's former
level-5 recording. sunken-court, fox-shrine, aurora-throne, dusk-belfry and
ember-crown (without -suno-v1) are the former synthesized expansion loops,
rebuildable with scripts/build-expansion-assets.py --music. They are no longer
selected by src/levels.ts. The synthesis script does not overwrite Suno exports.

Sound effects
-------------
Every effect (drawing, capture, the seal, the boss, UI clicks) is still
synthesized at runtime by src/audio.ts with the Web Audio API. Those
effects are original and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
