封林 audio
============

All music and sound effects are synthesized at runtime by src/audio.ts
with the Web Audio API. The repository does not ship audio files.

Source: original work written for this game. No samples, stems, or
melodies were taken from a recording or a sample library.

License: CC0 1.0 Universal
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
The synthesis code is dedicated to the public domain. The rest of the
game keeps whatever license the repository already uses.
