封林 audio
============

Background music
----------------
Each level loops one track supplied by the repository owner. The game
prefers the Opus .ogg and falls back to .mp3. A file is downloaded only
after the first tap or key press, and only for the level being played.

Level 1 — 月夜森林 Moonlit Grove (3:19)
  public/audio/moonlit-grove.ogg and public/audio/moonlit-grove.mp3
  Suno id 0bcc47c6-393b-4e6c-ad0b-e7c9d5b8aa77, created 2026-11-10.

Level 2 — Desert Temple (~1:17)
  public/audio/desert-temple.ogg and public/audio/desert-temple.mp3

Level 3 — Storm Laboratory (~1:33)
  public/audio/storm-lab.ogg and public/audio/storm-lab.mp3

Level 4 — Rose Waltz (~2:53)
  public/audio/rose-waltz.ogg and public/audio/rose-waltz.mp3

Level 5 — Autumn Ride (~1:50)
  public/audio/autumn-ride.ogg and public/audio/autumn-ride.mp3

These are the owner's own generations for this game. They are not
covered by the CC0 dedication below.

Sound effects
-------------
Every effect (drawing, capture, the seal, the boss, UI clicks) is still
synthesized at runtime by src/audio.ts with the Web Audio API. Those
effects are original and dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
